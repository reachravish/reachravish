# Customer Insights Analyst — Interview Prep Kit

Prep material built from the Customer Insights Analyst job description (customer / transaction / product / loyalty / marketing analytics + AI, CDP, and modern data platforms).

| File | What it is | Time |
|---|---|---|
| [`01-tutorial.md`](01-tutorial.md) | 2-hour structured tutorial: KPIs, SQL, segmentation, propensity models, campaign measurement, GenAI, CDP, dashboards & storytelling | 120 min |
| [`02-mock-interview.md`](02-mock-interview.md) | 50 mock interview questions with model answers, grouped by interview round | 60–90 min to rehearse |

## Suggested plan
1. **Day 1** – Work through the tutorial end-to-end (run the SQL/Python snippets if you can).
2. **Day 2** – Answer the mock questions out loud *before* reading the model answers. Time yourself (2 min per answer).
3. **Day 3** – Prepare 4–5 STAR stories from your own experience mapped to the JD (see the story map at the end of the mock interview file) and do the take-home case in Module 10.
